What does OCR do?

Optical Character Recognition reads the pixels of a scanned page and writes the recognised characters back into the PDF as an invisible text layer. The page looks identical, but you can now search, select and copy the words.

How to use it in 3 steps

  1. Upload your scanned PDF (max 20 MB and 30 pages).
  2. Choose the document language and what to download.
  3. Press Start OCR, then download the searchable PDF or the TXT file.

Frequently asked questions

Which languages are supported? {% if ocr_lang_count %}{{ ocr_lang_count }} language packs are installed on this server. Pick one, or two for a mixed-language document. English is the default.{% else %} English and Urdu.{% endif %}

Can I use two languages at once? Yes — set a second language for documents that mix scripts (for example English with Urdu). Only two are allowed per run, because each extra pack multiplies memory and processing time.

Why is there a page limit? OCR is memory hungry. Pages are processed one at a time at 200 DPI and capped at 30 pages so the server does not run out of memory.

My Urdu text looks reordered. Arabic-script text is stored in the correct reading order, but some PDF viewers re-render the shaped forms differently. The TXT download is the most reliable way to reuse the text.

Are my files kept? No. Uploads and results are deleted after download or automatically afterwards.
