Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Summary

PDFMaster is a free online PDF toolkit. The short version: files you upload are processed to give you the result you asked for, downloaded by you, and then deleted automatically. We do not sell personal data, and we do not read your documents.

2. Files you upload

  • Files are stored temporarily on the server for processing only.
  • They are removed automatically after processing or download.
  • Results are served through an unguessable job ID link.
  • Files are never shared with third parties and never used to train models.

3. Information we collect

  • Account data (optional): a username and password if you register - nothing else is required.
  • Technical logs: IP address, user agent and timestamps written by the web server for security and abuse prevention.
  • Usage events: which tool was used and whether it succeeded, kept in aggregate to prioritise fixes.

4. Cookies

We use only a session cookie required for the site to function (login state, CSRF protection, download links). Analytics and advertising cookies are set only if you accept them via the cookie banner. See the Cookie Policy for details.

5. Advertising

We may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings. Ads are disabled entirely until you consent where consent is required.

6. Legal basis (GDPR)

File processing is based on your explicit request (performance of a contract/legitimate interest); cookies beyond strictly necessary ones are based on consent, which you can withdraw at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

7. Retention

Uploaded files and generated results are deleted automatically within hours of use. Server logs are rotated and retained for up to 30 days. Account records persist until you delete your account.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold, and you may object to processing. Email support@pdfmaster.example and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

PDFMaster is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

Material changes will be announced on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

Questions? Contact us.
