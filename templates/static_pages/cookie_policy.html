Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Strictly necessary cookies

These keep the site working and are always on:

  • sessionid - keeps you logged in.
  • csrftoken - protects forms against cross-site request forgery.
  • download stash - temporary server-side state so ZIP/extract results can be re-downloaded without re-uploading.

2. Optional cookies

Analytics and advertising cookies (including Google AdSense) are set only after you press "Accept" in the cookie banner. Until then, no advertising or analytics cookies are stored.

3. How long they live

  • Session cookies expire when you close the browser or log out.
  • Consent cookies last 6 months, after which we ask again.
  • Third-party cookies follow the third party's own schedule.

4. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will break login and downloads.

5. Changes & contact

We will update this page if the cookies we use change. Questions go through the contact page.
