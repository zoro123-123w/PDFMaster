About PDFMaster

PDFMaster started as an internal utility for handling the paperwork that piles up around every project: invoices, scans, reports, forms. We wanted one page where the whole team could merge, split, compress, convert and search PDFs without installing heavyweight desktop software or sending documents to yet another subscription service.

What we offer

Every tool runs in your browser and does its work on our server in seconds - no queues, no sign-up required for the core tools. From merging PDFs and shrinking file sizes to making scans searchable with OCR, the goal is the same: the fastest path from "I need this done" to a downloaded file.

Privacy by default

Files you upload are processed, delivered back to you, then deleted automatically. We never ask for your documents to train models, never sell data, and never keep files around "just in case". Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

Free and sustainable

The core toolkit is free forever. We fund hosting and development through unobtrusive advertising and an optional pro tier with larger limits and priority processing.

Open to feedback

Found a bug or need a tool we don't have? Head to the contact page - messages go straight to the people who build the product.
