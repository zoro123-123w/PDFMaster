Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDFMaster free?

Yes! All our basic PDF tools are completely free to use. There are no hidden fees or subscriptions required.

How large can my PDF be?

Free users can upload PDFs up to 25 MB. Pro users will have access to larger file limits.

How do I merge PDFs?

Go to the Merge PDF tool, upload your PDF files, and click Merge. You can download the combined file instantly.

How do I compress a PDF?

Upload your PDF to the Compress PDF tool. We'll optimize it and show you the compression results before downloading.

Can I convert JPG to PDF?

Yes! Use our JPG to PDF tool. Upload your images and we'll combine them into a single PDF document.

Are my uploaded files stored?

No. All uploaded files are processed temporarily and automatically deleted after processing. We do not store your files.

Can I use PDFMaster on mobile?

Yes! PDFMaster is fully responsive and works on all devices including phones and tablets.

Will more PDF tools be added?

Yes! We're constantly working on adding new tools. Check back often for updates.
